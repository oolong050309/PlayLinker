About This Game

Apex Legends is a free-to-play hero shooter game where legendary competitors battle for glory, fame, and fortune on the fringes of the Frontier. Play as unique Legends with powerful abilities and work with your squadmates to become the last team standing.

Choose from a diverse cast of Legends, each with their own unique personality, strengths, and abilities. Master your Legend's abilities, make strategic decisions on the fly, and use your team's strengths to your advantage.

Experience intense combat in a variety of maps and game modes. From the classic Battle Royale to limited-time events, there's always something new to discover in the Apex Games.
